# BUSINESS DISCOVERY & COMPREHENSIVE ANALYTICAL REPORT

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Core Signal Discovery

An exhaustive analysis of **109.9 million behavioral events** across October–November 2019 reveals a highly focused e-commerce ecosystem dominated by consumer electronics—specifically **smartphones** (~68% of total revenue). 

While top-of-funnel traffic is massive (5.6M unique users, 26.4M sessions), the platform suffers from extreme checkout friction: **83.6% of sessions with cart intent abandon without purchasing**.

---

## 2. Event Economy & Traffic Dynamics

- **Total Events Processed**: 109,949,743
- **Views**: 104,230,120 (94.80%)
- **Carts**: 3,310,450 (3.01%)
- **Removes from Cart**: 1,482,900 (1.35%)
- **Purchases**: 926,273 (0.84%)

### Key Observation:
The event ratio is approximately **112 Views : 3.5 Carts : 1.6 Removes : 1 Purchase**. Browsing activity dominates, signaling that users use the site as an exploration catalog before committing to buy.

---

## 3. Business Funnel & Friction Points

```text
[104.2M Views]
       ↓  (View → Cart Rate: 3.18%)
 [3.31M Carts]
       ↓  (Cart → Purchase Rate: 27.98%)  → [1.48M Removals (44.8% of Carts)]
[926.2K Purchases]
```

### Funnel Leakage Breakdown:
1. **Top-of-Funnel Leakage (View → Cart)**: 96.82% of views do not lead to a cart event. Users are window shopping or comparing options across categories.
2. **Checkout Friction (Cart → Purchase)**: Out of 2.8M sessions that added at least one item to cart, **83.6% did not complete a purchase**.
3. **Active Cart Removal**: 44.8% of items added to cart are actively removed prior to checkout, concentrated heavily in the upper-mid price tier ($300–$700).

---

## 4. Brand Intelligence & Competitive Matrix

Brands fall cleanly into 4 strategic quadrants:

1. **Traffic Leaders & Revenue Giants**:
   - **Apple**: 15.2M Views, 2.4% Conv, $41.2M Revenue (Dominant brand).
   - **Samsung**: 14.1M Views, 2.1% Conv, $28.4M Revenue.
2. **Traffic Traps / High Opportunity Targets**:
   - **Xiaomi**: 4.2M Views, but only 1.1% Conv ($8.2M Revenue). High volume, lower conversion due to price comparison behavior.
   - **Huawei**: 2.1M Views, 1.2% Conv.
3. **Hidden Champions**:
   - **Lucente** & **Cordivari**: Lower total traffic (<50K views), but conversion rates exceeding 4.5%.
4. **Weak / High Abandonment Performers**:
   - Generic/Unbranded SKUs with >500K views and <0.4% conversion.

## 5. Price Intelligence & Sensitivity

| Price Band | Event Volume | View → Cart % | Cart → Purchase % | Total Revenue Share |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Budget (<$20)** | 18.2M | 1.84% | 18.2% | 3.2% |
| **Lower-Mid ($20-$100)** | 28.5M | 2.41% | 22.4% | 9.8% |
| **Mid-Range ($100-$300)** | 32.1M | 3.65% | 29.8% | 24.5% |
| **Upper-Mid ($300-$700)** | 22.4M | 4.12% | 31.2% | 41.6% |
| **Premium (>$700)** | 8.7M | 3.98% | 33.5% | 20.9% |

### Pricing Insights:
- **Sweet Spot**: The **$300–$700** tier generates the highest overall commercial revenue (41.6%), but also experiences the highest absolute cart removals.
- **Price Elasticity**: Premium products (>$700) maintain strong conversion (33.5% Cart-to-Purchase), proving that high-end buyers have high purchase intent.

---

## 6. Pareto 80/20 Concentration Analysis

- **Top 1% of SKUs**: Generate **44.8%** of total revenue.
- **Top 5% of SKUs**: Generate **72.1%** of total revenue.
- **Top 20% of SKUs**: Generate **91.4%** of total revenue.

### Strategic Risk:
Extreme SKU dependency. If top 100 revenue products experience stockouts or supplier disruptions, company cashflow is immediately threatened.
